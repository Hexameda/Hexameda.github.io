Frequency · Fremonia

Privacy, without interrupting your calm.

How the Frequency wellness app handles information on your device and through the services needed for ads and purchases.

Last updated · 16 November 2026

In short: Your journal, wellness check-ins, profile details, custom routines and profile photo stay on your device. We do not sell them or send them to advertising systems. Ads and in-app purchases rely on the limited third-party services described below.

1. Who we are and scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Frequency mobile application (also presented as “Fremonia” in certain service dashboards), operated by the Frequency development team (“Frequency”, “we”, “us”). Frequency is a wellness and focus application and is not a medical service.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded by Frequency to our own servers:

  • your chosen name, age and profile photo;
  • journal entries and local journal analyses;
  • sleep, stress and energy check-ins;
  • custom frequencies, listening history, session state, calendar routines and reminders;
  • language, appearance, notification and other app preferences; and
  • local ad pacing and temporary rewarded ad-free status.

Profile photos are copied into the app’s private local storage. Calendar reminders use local notifications. Frequency does not obtain a remote push notification token for marketing messages.

3. Advertising and consent

If you have not purchased ad-free access, Frequency uses Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (“UMP”) to provide banner, interstitial and, for adults, optional rewarded ads. Frequency requests non-personalized ads. Google may still process limited information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, consent choices and ad impressions or clicks to deliver contextual ads, prevent fraud, limit repetition, measure performance and comply with law.

Your exact age is kept on your device. Frequency uses it to determine age-appropriate ad handling and sends Google only the applicable under-age-of-consent treatment signal. Rewarded ads are not offered to users identified as under 18. You can revisit required privacy choices from the “Manage privacy choices” control in the app.

4. Purchases and ad-free access

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Frequency does not receive or store your full payment card or bank details. RevenueCat helps us retrieve product information, validate purchases and maintain the ad-free entitlement. For this purpose, RevenueCat may process an app-generated anonymous user identifier, purchase and entitlement information, and limited device and app information.

6. Legal bases and international processing

Where applicable, we rely on your consent for consent-based advertising activities, performance of the purchase contract for paid features, and our legitimate interests in operating the app safely, preventing fraud and maintaining basic functionality. Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own and apply their own legal safeguards for international transfers.

7. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, use the app’s reset-data control or uninstall the app. Deleting local data does not cancel or erase App Store transaction records or the purchase entitlement needed to restore ad-free access. Google, RevenueCat and Apple retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal information, or withdraw consent. You can change eligible advertising consent choices inside the app. For data controlled directly by Google, RevenueCat or Apple, you may also use the privacy controls and contact channels in their policies.

9. Children and teenagers

Frequency is not intended for children under 13 and onboarding does not accept an age below 13. Users aged 13–17 receive restricted, non-personalized and age-appropriate ad treatment; optional rewarded ads are disabled for them. A parent or guardian who believes a child under 13 has used the app should contact us.

10. Security and policy changes

We use platform protections and limit data flows to what is needed for app functionality, advertising and purchases. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We may update this policy as the app or legal requirements change; the latest version and effective date will remain available at this address.

11. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact the Frequency development team by opening a private-data-free support request at github.com/Hexameda/frequency/issues. Please do not include health entries, payment information or other sensitive data in a public issue.

Yerel uygulama verileri siz silene, uygulamadaki veri sıfırlama seçeneğini kullanana veya uygulamayı kaldırana kadar cihazınızda kalır. Google, RevenueCat ve Apple bilgileri kendi politikaları ve yasal yükümlülükleri doğrultusunda saklar.

8. Gizlilik haklarınız

Yaşadığınız yere bağlı olarak kişisel bilgilere erişme, bunları düzeltme veya silme, işlemeyi kısıtlama ya da işleme itiraz etme ve rızayı geri çekme haklarına sahip olabilirsiniz. Uygun reklam rızası seçimlerini uygulama içinden değiştirebilirsiniz. Doğrudan Google, RevenueCat veya Apple tarafından kontrol edilen veriler için ilgili gizlilik politikalarındaki denetimleri ve iletişim kanallarını da kullanabilirsiniz.

9. Çocuklar ve gençler

Frequency 13 yaşın altındaki çocuklara yönelik değildir ve başlangıç adımı 13’ten küçük bir yaşı kabul etmez. 13–17 yaşındaki kullanıcılara kısıtlı, kişiselleştirilmemiş ve yaşa uygun reklam işlemi uygulanır; isteğe bağlı ödüllü reklamlar bu kullanıcılar için kapatılır. 13 yaşın altındaki bir çocuğun uygulamayı kullandığını düşünen ebeveyn veya vasiler bizimle iletişime geçmelidir.
